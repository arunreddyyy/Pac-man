A horror-themed Pac-Man-style maze game that runs in the browser. Eat every pellet, grab the glowing orbs to turn the ghosts into prey, and survive as the maze gets harder. Written in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, so there is nothing to install.
controls W,A,S,D to move the character and P to pause the game and other thing is we can play this on tablet or phone the controls are just touch or slide the way you wanted to go.
How to play
Pellets (small dots): 10 points each.
Glow orbs (spinning diamonds): 75 points. They scare every ghost for a limited time, and you can eat scared ghosts.
Cherry: appears occasionally for a short time, worth 500 points, and also scares the ghosts.
Eating a scared ghost: 250 points. The ghost turns into eyes and returns to its starting box, then comes back.
Clearing a level: eat all pellets and all glow orbs. A new random maze is generated for each level.
Ghosts get faster each level (up to a cap), and a 4th ghost joins from level 3.
Scatter phase: for about 2 seconds at the start of every level, and after every life lost, the ghosts wander instead of hunting you.
Scared ghosts flash white just before they turn dangerous again, so you know when to run.
There are different types of themes and difficulty in the game you can select what ever you  want.
